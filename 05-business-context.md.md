**Domain:**

Retail





**Business Problem:**



A retail company is experiencing uneven sales performance across regions and product categories, along with declining profitability in certain high-selling products due to discounting and cost pressures.


Despite increasing revenue in some segments, overall profit margins are not improving proportionally. The company also lacks visibility into which products and regions are truly driving sustainable growth versus those relying heavily on discounts or low-margin sales.


The management wants to use data analytics to:


•	Identify key drivers of sales and profitability

•	Understand product, region, and customer segment performance

•	Optimize pricing and discount strategies

•	Improve inventory allocation and reduce inefficiencies


The goal is to increase revenue quality (not just volume) and improve overall profitability.







**Questions for the Business Problem:**



**1. Sales \& Profitability Drivers**

•	Which product categories and regions contribute most to sales and profit?

**2. Regional \& Segment Analysis**

•	Which regions are high-performing in terms of both sales and profit?

•	Which customer segments generate the highest Profit Margin?

**3. Product Performance Analysis**

•	Which product categories have high sales but low profit margins?

•	Which products are underperforming despite high demand?

**4. Pricing \& Discount Impact**

•	How do discounts impact sales volume and profit margins?

•	Are there products where discounts increase sales but reduce overall profitability?

**5. Structural Dependency \& Risk**

•	Which regions are overly dependent on a single product category?

•	Is there concentration risk in specific products or categories?

**6. Optimization \& Recommendations**

•	What actions can improve profitability without reducing sales volume?

•	How should inventory and discount strategies be optimized across regions and categories?
