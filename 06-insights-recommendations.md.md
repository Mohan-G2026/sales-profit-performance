**Executive Summary**





* Profitability is uneven: Furniture drives high sales (\~32%) but delivers very low margins (\~2%), driven by higher discounting and a narrow Sales–Profit gap,  
suggesting elevated effective cost pressure.



* Discounting is the key margin driver: profitability declines sharply beyond 20% discount levels, while low/no discount strategies generate the highest margins.



* Regional imbalance exists: West leads in both sales and profitability, while Central underperforms due to higher discount intensity.



* Growth is concentrated: Technology is the strongest category driver, particularly in East and South regions.



* Revenue concentration is moderate: the top 10 subcategories contribute \~85% of total sales, indicating moderate dependence on a limited number of subcategories.







**Data limitations**



“Due to lack of cost-level granularity, profit decomposition is limited to discount impact.”







**Insights**





* Furniture drives \~32% of sales but contributes only \~2% margin, primarily due to heavy discounting (\~17% vs others 11-15%), especially in Tables (21%) and Bookcases (18%) vs others 9-15%, along with narrow Sales–Profit gaps indicating elevated effective cost pressure. Office Supplies, despite similar sales contribution, delivers significantly
higher margins (\~17%).



* Central region underperforms (\~8% margin) despite strong sales due to higher discount intensity (\~16% vs others 13-14%), while West leads (\~15% margin) with more controlled pricing behavior. Key segments such as Central–Consumer and South–Home Office show margin erosion driven by over-discounting and narrow Sales–Profit gaps, indicating weaker cost-to-sales efficiency in these areas.



* Profitability declines sharply beyond \~20% discount levels, with extreme discounting leading to negative or near-zero returns, while low-discount transactions consistently generate stronger margins with limited incremental volume benefit.



* Sales are broadly balanced across categories; however, Technology is the strongest growth driver (\~38–39%), particularly in East and South regions, while Central remains stable but less growth-oriented.



* Revenue is moderately concentrated, with the top 5 of 17 subcategories contributing \~55% of sales and the top 10 contributing \~85%\*\*, indicating reliance on a limited set of subcategories, while the remaining subcategories collectively contribute \~15%.







**Recommendations**





* Strengthen discount governance, particularly in Furniture, Central region, and high-impact segments (Tables, Bookcases, Central–Consumer, South–Home Office)



* Benchmark discounting and pricing discipline against West region to improve margin consistency



* Shift from blanket discounts to targeted, value-based promotional strategies, especially for low elasticity segments



* Investigate cost-to-sales efficiency in low Sales–Profit gap segments to identify operational improvement opportunities



* Cap discount levels at \~20% and monitor margin impact before applying higher promotions



* Expand focus on high-margin categories (Office Supplies, Home Office) to balance portfolio profitability



* Accelerate growth of Technology category in East and South regions



* Reduce revenue dependency on top products through bundling, rationalization, and promotion of mid-tier subcategories







**Strategic Actions \& Business Optimization**





* Shift from volume-led growth to margin-led growth strategy, prioritizing profitability over sales expansion.



* Implement structured discount governance, applying controlled and performance-based discounting rather than blanket promotions.



* Optimize product portfolio by reducing focus on low-performing products and strengthening high-margin offerings.



* Strengthen regional profitability strategy by reducing excessive discounting in low-performing regions and reinforcing high-performing regions like West.



* Introduce a KPI framework focused on:
1. &#x20;     Margin %
2. &#x20;     Discount-to-Sales Ratio
3. &#x20;     Contribution Margin
4. &#x20;     Revenue Quality Index
